VetAssist Quick Help & Guide

Which mode should I choose?

🩺 Forward Chaining: "I know the symptoms - tell me what it could be."
Pick your animal, check the signs you see, and we check all rules to see what conditions match.
💬 Backward Chaining: "I suspect a disease - ask me questions to check it."
Pick a condition you are worried about, and we ask you step-by-step questions to prove or rule it out.

🚨 When to seek immediate emergency care

If your pet is struggling to breathe, unable to pee, has a hard swollen abdomen with retching, continuous bloody vomiting/diarrhea, or has collapsed, do NOT wait for an online tool. Rush to the nearest 24/7 veterinary hospital immediately.

Understanding Confidence

Confidence shows how strongly your symptom pattern points to a possible condition. It is a guide to assist veterinary discussion, not a guarantee.
